About

I'm a master's student in the Quantitative Methods track of the MAPSS program at the University of Chicago. I graduated from Bates College in 2026 with a B.A. in Economics, minors in Mathematics and Digital & Computational Studies, Phi Beta Kappa, and I spent a year studying at the London School of Economics.

My work sits where econometrics meets data science: building datasets from APIs and the web, turning text into signals, and testing what those signals say about markets and behavior. I'm especially interested in quantitative finance, and I serve as Professional Outreach Chair for the Phoenix Quant Collective at UChicago.

Research

Reddit Sentiment and AI Product Announcements

2025
Senior thesis, Bates College

Built a daily firm-level sentiment signal from ~15,000 Reddit posts and comments and tested whether retail-investor sentiment predicts abnormal stock returns around AI product announcements. Sentiment Granger-causes next-day abnormal returns (but not the reverse), and VAR impulse responses show the effect fades within about three days.

Crowdfunding Campaigns at Scale
